Services/SEO Services
SEO & Performance

SEO Services

We get your site ranking for the searches that actually convert — not just the ones with the highest volume. Technical fixes, content structure, and link building, tracked back to organic traffic and leads.

Why this matters

Most businesses only rank for their own brand name and nothing else — which means every buyer who doesn't already know you never finds you. Real growth comes from ranking for what people search before they've heard of your brand: "best X near me," "X vs Y," "how to choose X." SEO is also one of the few marketing assets that compounds — a page that ranks well keeps sending traffic for years without a fresh budget every month. But getting there takes real technical and content work, not a metadata tweak and a prayer.

How we run it

We start with a full technical audit — crawl errors, broken indexing, slow pages, missing schema — because no amount of content fixes a site Google can't properly read. From there it's keyword strategy tied to buyer intent, content built to actually earn the click and hold attention, and off-page authority from real, relevant sites, not spam link farms. You get a monthly report on rankings and the traffic and leads that follow — not a screenshot of keyword positions with no context.

  • Technical SEO audit & fixes
  • Keyword & content strategy
  • On-page optimization
  • Link building & authority growth
  • Monthly ranking & traffic reports

AEO Services

Search is splitting between Google and AI answer engines. AEO structures your content so tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google's AI Overviews cite your brand directly in the answer, not buried three links down.

Why this matters

A growing share of searches never reach a results page at all — they get answered directly inside ChatGPT, Perplexity, or a Google AI Overview. If your brand isn't the source cited in that answer, you don't exist in it, no matter how well you'd have ranked the old way. AEO isn't classic SEO with a new label; it's about structuring facts, specs, and FAQs so an AI system can lift them cleanly and attribute them back to you.

How we run it

We identify the actual questions your buyers are asking AI tools about your category, then restructure your key pages into clean, extractable answer blocks — proper schema markup, FAQ formatting, clear factual statements instead of marketing fluff. Alongside that we build the entity signals (consistent naming, structured data, cross-referenced mentions) that make AI systems trust you as a source in the first place, and we actually check whether you're showing up inside AI answers rather than assuming it worked.

  • Structured content for AI answer boxes
  • Schema & entity markup
  • FAQ and answer-format content
  • Citation tracking across AI engines
  • Monthly visibility report

Generative Engine Optimization

GEO is how your brand shows up inside AI-generated answers, summaries, and recommendations — not just search results. We format and place content so generative engines pull your brand in as the source.

Why this matters

AEO gets you cited in a direct answer. GEO is the wider game — getting recommended inside AI-generated summaries, comparisons, and "best of" lists a generative engine assembles on its own. It's the modern equivalent of landing on a buyer's shortlist before they've even opened a search engine, and most brands haven't touched it yet.

How we run it

We research how large language models currently describe your category and your competitors, then build content specifically shaped to be retrievable and quotable by those models. We also strengthen the web-wide entity signals that make an LLM associate your brand with the right terms — and because this space moves fast, we re-check visibility regularly instead of treating it as a one-time setup, since what worked last quarter can go stale as models update.

  • Content formatted for LLM retrieval
  • Brand entity building across the web
  • Prompt-pattern research for your category
  • Ongoing GEO visibility tracking

Performance Marketing

Every rupee tracked back to a lead or a sale. We run paid campaigns across Meta, Google, and beyond with one goal — a lower cost per result than you're getting today.

Why this matters

Performance marketing exists to answer one question honestly: what did this spend actually return? Too many campaigns get judged on reach and impressions — numbers that look good on a slide but don't pay rent. If a campaign can't be traced back to a lead or a sale, it isn't performance marketing, it's just advertising with extra steps.

How we run it

We start with the number that actually matters to you — cost per lead, cost per sale, or ROAS — and reverse-engineer the campaign structure to hit it. Creative and audiences get tested aggressively in the first two weeks; whatever doesn't work gets cut fast, and whatever does gets scaled. Everything ties back to that one number in a weekly report you can read in two minutes, not a 40-tab spreadsheet.

  • Campaign strategy & audience targeting
  • Creative testing at scale
  • Landing page optimization
  • Daily budget & bid management
  • Weekly performance reporting

Search Engine Marketing

Paid search that shows up exactly when someone's ready to buy. We manage bids, keywords, and ad copy on Google Ads so your budget goes to searches that convert.

Why this matters

SEM is the fastest lever available — someone searching "X near me" right now can be a customer today. It's also the easiest way to quietly burn a budget on clicks that never convert if the account is left to run on broad match and good intentions.

How we run it

Tight keyword match types instead of broad-match guesswork, negative keywords added weekly to cut wasted spend before it adds up, and ad copy tested in pairs so we actually know which line is pulling its weight. Just as important — we check where the click lands. Paid traffic sent to a generic homepage converts far worse than traffic sent to a page built to match what was searched, so landing pages get fixed or built as part of the work, not treated as someone else's problem.

  • Keyword & bid strategy
  • Ad copy & extensions
  • Conversion tracking setup
  • Negative keyword management
  • Monthly spend & ROI review

We find creators whose audience actually matches yours, negotiate the deal, and manage delivery — so you get content and reach, not just an invoice.

Why this matters

The most common influencer marketing failure is depressingly simple: a creator with a big following posts once, gets paid, and the brand never hears from that audience again — because the fit was wrong from the start. UGC Video

Native-feeling video that looks like it came from a real customer, not a brand — because that's the format that actually stops the scroll on Instagram and TikTok.

Why this matters

Viewers can tell within the first second whether something is a polished brand ad or a real person talking — and on Instagram and TikTok specifically, the second one gets watched longer. Stopping the scroll is the entire game; a beautifully lit ad that gets skipped in half a second has achieved nothing.

How we run it

We write the hook and structure like a real review, not a script read off a teleprompter. Depending on the brief, we either cast a creator who can deliver it like they mean it, or shoot it in-house. Editing follows the platform's native pacing rather than TV-ad pacing, and we produce multiple hook variants so we can actually test which opening earns the first three seconds instead of guessing.

  • Script & concept development
  • Creator casting or in-house shoot
  • Editing for platform-native feel
  • Multiple hook variants for testing

WhatsApp API Services

The official WhatsApp Business API, set up and run for you — broadcast to customers, automate replies, and sell directly inside the chat app your customers already have open. This is the service Waptrix was built around, and it's the one most of our clients start with.

Why this matters

WhatsApp is where your customers already are — checked more often than email, and often more than Instagram. But the official Business API is a different animal from the WhatsApp app on your phone: it needs proper Meta verification, approved message templates, and correct setup before you can send a single broadcast. Get any of that wrong and you're stuck waiting on a rejected template while a launch date passes.

How we run it

We handle the Meta verification process end to end, and set up message templates and catalogs correctly the first time — a rejected template can cost you days you don't have. We build the automated flows that actually answer common questions instead of dead-ending in a bot loop nobody can escape, and connect it to your CRM or store so a WhatsApp conversation can turn into an order without anyone copy-pasting between five apps.

  • WhatsApp Business API setup & green-tick verification
  • Broadcast campaigns, catalogs & template messages
  • Automated chatbot & auto-reply flows
  • Order and lead capture inside WhatsApp
  • Integration with your CRM, Shopify, or website
