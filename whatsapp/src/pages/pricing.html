Meta charges

What you pay Meta.

On the official API, Meta charges for the template messages businesses send. Rates depend on the message type and the customer's country, and Meta updates them from time to time — we pass them on at cost.

Message typeUsed forCharged?
MarketingOffers, announcements, re-engaging old leadsYes, per message
UtilityOrder updates, reminders, payment confirmationsYes, per message — free when replying within an open chat
AuthenticationOne-time passwords and login codesYes, per message
Service repliesYour replies within 24 hours of the customer's last messageFree
